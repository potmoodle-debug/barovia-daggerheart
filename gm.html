GM METHOD

Running threads

1. Situation, not quest. Record what is happening and who cares about it.

2. Pressure moves. Decide what changes if nobody interferes.

3. Evidence appears. Let the players see consequences before forcing confrontation.

4. Outcomes become canon. Once the party changes a thread, update the people and places it touches.
